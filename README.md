A two-sentence horror story for the make.fun "Two-Sentence Horror" bounty: a grieving father keeps a nightly ritual alive through his dead daughter's nursery monitor, until one night the ritual answers back. `main.py` (pure Python standard library, no dependencies, no network) writes the story to the path in `ENTRY_OUTPUT`, defaulting to `/output/entry.txt`.

> Every night since the funeral, I read my daughter a bedtime story through the nursery monitor, because the grief counselor said a ritual would help me let go. Last night I stopped mid-sentence, and a small voice — hers, word-perfect — finished it from inside her empty room.
